Refund Policy

01

How to Cancel

Travel bookings can be cancelled at any time by contacting our support team directly. We will guide you through the process and confirm the cancellation as quickly as possible.

02

Cancellation Charges

Cancellation fees may apply and vary based on the following factors:

Airline policies
Hotel policies
Tour operator rules
Time of cancellation
03

Service Charges

Travelog may deduct applicable service charges along with any third-party cancellation fees at the time of processing. The final refundable amount will be communicated once the cancellation is confirmed with all providers.
