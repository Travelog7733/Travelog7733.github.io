Cancellation Policy

💳

Refund Processing

Refunds will be processed only after receiving confirmation from airlines, hotels, or other relevant service providers. Once confirmation is received, we initiate the refund promptly.

⏱

Processing Time

Refund processing may take between 7–14 working days depending on your payment method and the policies of the respective service provider. We appreciate your patience during this time.

🚫

Non-Refundable Items

Certain bookings or promotional packages may be strictly non-refundable. Additionally, service charges paid directly to Travelog may not be eligible for a refund. Please review your booking details carefully before confirming.
