Privacy Policy

Your travel details stay protected.

Travelog collects only the information needed to plan, confirm, and support your trip. This policy explains how we handle your details with care.

Policy Summary

We use customer information for travel planning, booking support, communication, and service improvement.

Introduction

Travelog respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website, contact us, or book travel services with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that helps us understand your travel requirement and provide the right service.

  • Name and contact details
  • Email address and phone number
  • Travel preferences, destinations, dates, and booking details
  • Passport, visa, or document details only when required for a booking service
  • Basic website usage information

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to plan trips, process bookings, coordinate support, and keep you updated about your travel arrangements.

  • Prepare customized tour packages and itineraries
  • Process travel bookings and related services
  • Provide customer support before, during, and after travel
  • Improve our services and destination recommendations
  • Send important travel updates or relevant offers

Data Protection

Travelog uses reasonable security measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. We only share details with trusted partners when needed to complete travel-related services, such as hotels, transfers, visa support, or tour operations.

Contact Information

For privacy questions, data requests, booking support, or corrections to your information, contact Travelog directly.
